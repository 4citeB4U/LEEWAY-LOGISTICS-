# Transportation Verticals

## Shared core

Every customer receives tenant administration, users and roles, fleet and vehicle management, drivers, maintenance, inspections, incidents, documents, notifications, dashboards, reporting, integrations, audit history, training, and Agent Lee assistance.

## Trucking and freight

Loads, shipments, tractor and trailer assignments, driver qualifications, pre-trip and post-trip inspections, ELD integration, fuel and mileage, proof of delivery, maintenance compliance, and freight customer cases. The product will not claim certified ELD status without completing the applicable certification and registration process.

## School transportation

Districts, schools, routes, stops, student rosters, boarding and drop-off confirmation, guardian notifications, substitute-driver workflows, service interruptions, safety reports, and camera-event review. Student and guardian data require enhanced privacy controls.

## Passenger transit, shuttle, and paratransit

Fixed routes, scheduled trips, paratransit requests, passenger cases, service alerts, dispatch intervention, accessibility requirements, incident response, and operator assignments.

## Delivery and service fleets

Routes, stops, jobs, proof of delivery or service, vehicle utilization, customer notifications, photo evidence, and exception handling.
