# Interview Assessment Source Authority

## Source-grounded design

- Microsoft Learn Practice Assessments are preparation tools that expose question style and identify areas needing more preparation; they do not replace training or hands-on experience.
- Microsoft Applied Skills score reports separate pass/fail, skill-area performance, and recommended remediation.
- NIST role-based training guidance requires training that is tailored to assigned duties and updated when systems change.
- NIST training-record guidance supports documenting and monitoring training activity and retaining individual records.

## LeeWay implementation

The private assessment package therefore includes answer-free learner quizzes, practical labs, a separate instructor answer key, skill-category scoring, remediation, progress records, and an every-phase release gate. Commercial customer training remains separate and does not receive Leonard's answer keys or private progress.
