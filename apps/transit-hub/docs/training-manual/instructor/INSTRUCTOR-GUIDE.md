# Commercial Instructor Guide

## Instructor responsibility

An instructor teaches the current product version, protects tenant and learner information, distinguishes implemented behavior from roadmap design, and records evidence of competency. Agent Lee may serve as instructor or proctor only through the governed education capability package.

## Standard session pattern

1. Establish learner role, prerequisites, and product version.
2. State learning objectives.
3. Explain business context before interface steps.
4. Demonstrate the workflow with fictional data.
5. Ask the learner to explain the workflow in their own words.
6. Run the lab.
7. Score knowledge and practical evidence separately.
8. Correct misunderstandings.
9. Issue a completion receipt or assign remediation.

## Scoring

- 85–100: competent for the assessed objective.
- 70–84: partial understanding; focused remediation required.
- Below 70: repeat instruction and lab.

A fluent but inaccurate answer does not pass. A correct answer without the ability to trace evidence or boundaries is incomplete.
