# Commercial Learner Workbook

Use this workbook with the 24 initial manual chapters. For each chapter, record:

- chapter and product version;
- your role and tenant training context;
- three concepts learned;
- one request or workflow trace;
- one tenant, security, or safety boundary;
- the guided-lab result;
- questions requiring instructor or Agent Lee support;
- your final explanation in plain language.

## Reusable response frame

**What is the responsibility?**  
**Where does it belong?**  
**Why does it exist?**  
**What enters and leaves it?**  
**What may it change?**  
**What evidence proves it?**  
**What is still incomplete?**
