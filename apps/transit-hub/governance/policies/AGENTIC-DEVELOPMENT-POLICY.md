# Agentic Development Policy

Agents may propose plans, create code, write tests, and identify repairs. They may not self-approve changes.

A change is accepted only after:

- the changed files are identified;
- the purpose and data flow are explained;
- the build passes;
- the required tests pass;
- the diff is reviewed;
- security and database impact are classified;
- Leonard Lee approves the receipt.
