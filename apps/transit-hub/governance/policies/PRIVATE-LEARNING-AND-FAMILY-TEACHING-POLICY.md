# Private Learning and Family Teaching Policy

1. Private-learning material is excluded from commercial customer documentation output.
2. Leonard Lee is the default owner and learner.
3. Family learners require explicit delegation and may use aliases.
4. Child identity, school information, credentials, customer data, and sensitive scores are prohibited from public or customer artifacts.
5. Agent Lee must resolve private visibility before retrieving answers, progress, or instructor rubrics.
6. Answer rubrics remain hidden until an attempt is submitted unless Leonard Lee explicitly requests direct instruction.
7. Commercial manuals may reuse general teaching methods but not private responses or progress.
8. Every question, answer rubric, and lesson references the product version and supporting evidence.
