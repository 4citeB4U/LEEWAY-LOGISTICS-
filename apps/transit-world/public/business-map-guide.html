Find your controls

The left business sidebar opens Map, Loads, Drivers, Fleet, Transit, CRM, Features and Intelligence. Collapse makes the sidebar narrower. On a small screen, use Business menu to open it. The LeeWay logo remains in the header.

Directions plans a trip; My Location requests your device’s location. Allow location access when asked. If access fails, read the reason and retry after correcting the device or browser setting; the map does not invent your position.

The bottom controls open traffic, cameras, weather, street or satellite imagery, reports, driver radio, Agent Lee, language/music and 3D. Map settings contains Views (map appearance), Layers (data visibility) and Scenes (saved viewpoints). This printable guide is linked inside Map settings. Intelligence opens the compact layer list with source status.

Layer and symbol reference

Layer / symbolWhat it means and how to use it
Traffic • moving road dotsRoad activity visualization. Dot motion may be simulated; it does not identify individual cars. Where a live flow provider is available, road colors reflect its reported traffic conditions. Check the source/status before treating a color as current traffic.
Green flow
Amber flow
Red flow		On traffic-flow roads: free flow, slower traffic and congestion/jam respectively. These colors are not a universal legend for every map layer. White or unclassified road dots do not prove a clear road.
Camera icon • CCTVA mapped traffic camera. Select it to inspect the provider and image or video. A snapshot is a periodically refreshed picture; HLS is video. A camera marker alone does not prove a working or current feed. Read unavailable/stale messages and timestamps.
Transit routes • colored linesBus, tram, subway, rail or ferry routes. Colors may come from the transit agency. Mapped OSM route geometry describes the network; it is not a live schedule or evidence of a vehicle’s current location.
Cyan stop dots • regional transitA mapped stop. Select it for stop information and available departures. Some agencies require a configured provider key; unavailable arrival data must not be read as “no service.”
Orange vehicle dots • regional transitReported vehicle positions where a working feed exists. Read agency, timestamp and freshness. Other transit vehicle layers use mode colors: green bus, amber tram, red subway, purple rail, cyan ferry and gray unknown.
Incident markersRoad incidents from the active source, or separately labeled community reports. Select a marker to read the event, reported time and details; an orange incident marker does not indicate transit.
Weather • shaded areas and alertsWeather imagery and alert areas depend on the enabled layer. Read the alert title, severity, affected area and validity times. Color alone is insufficient; radar, alerts and visual weather effects represent different information.
Route line • DirectionsYour selected planned trip. Route alternatives can use different colors. Follow the route summary and travel mode. A planned line is not proof of current road conditions or commercial vehicle clearance.
Loads and business recordsLoads, Drivers, Fleet and CRM open business records and dispatch tools. These records belong to LeeWay Logistics, not Personal Maps. Load comparisons show separate pickup/delivery paths; read offer labels rather than assuming a universal route color.
World, street, satellite and 3DThese change your view or background. Satellite imagery is not live video. 3D availability and detail depend on the selected map source and loaded coverage.

Read status before acting

Enabled means a layer is selected, not that every provider is online. Loading means a request is pending. Unavailable may mean missing credentials, unsupported coverage or a failed request. Stale means older data is retained. Always check source attribution and the observation time where shown.

Turn layers off in Map settings → Layers to simplify the map. Keep only the information needed for the trip. Missing cameras, arrivals or weather observations must not be filled with invented live data. Different layers can reuse colors: use the marker’s label and details to interpret it.

Print and accessibility

Use Print / Save as PDF above, then select your printer or PDF destination. The print layout removes the print button and preserves the written legend, so the guide remains useful in black and white. Controls support keyboard focus; use Tab to move between controls and Enter or Space to activate a button. Agent Lee’s voice connection is optional for using the map.
