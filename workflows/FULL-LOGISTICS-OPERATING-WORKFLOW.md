# Full Logistics Operating Workflow

1. Recruit / onboard employee or carrier.
2. Validate credentials, equipment, insurance, authority, and required documents.
3. Add vehicle/trailer/facility/customer/broker records.
4. Discover or create a load.
5. Evaluate equipment match, HOS, appointments, deadhead, route risk, and economics.
6. Negotiate / confirm rate and contract.
7. Assign driver, tractor, trailer, and route.
8. Render the load and route in Transit World.
9. Gate the route against truck restrictions; UNVERIFIED is not SAFE.
10. Track progress, traffic, weather, public transit context, exceptions, and communications.
11. Handle detention, maintenance, incident, or reroute workflows.
12. Capture BOL/POD and settlement documents.
13. Close load, update CRM/account history, emit receipts, and retain evidence.
