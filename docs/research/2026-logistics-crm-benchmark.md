# 2026 Logistics / CRM Benchmark

## Market patterns recovered

### Large carrier / brokerage platform pattern
Modern carrier platforms centralize load search, booking/offers, load management, driver assignment, shipment tracking, document upload, check calls, facility information, payment workflows, and personalized load recommendations.

### Flexible CRM / work-management pattern
Modern configurable CRMs centralize accounts, contacts, activities, pipelines, forms, boards, connected records, dashboards, multiple views, workflow automation, notifications, and cross-team coordination.

## LeeWay synthesis

LeeWay Logistics should combine those patterns but change the primary interface from a dashboard-first product into a world-first spatial operating system.

The world is the shared index. CRM boards and operational workspaces remain available as alternate views over the same underlying records.

## Required workspaces

- Facilities / Terminals
- Municipal Transit
- HR

- Documents / Contracts
- Finance / Settlement
- Analytics / Reporting
- Formula / Veritas / Receipts

## Differentiator

A customer, broker, terminal, repair hub, employee location, vehicle, route, load, and facility can all be spatially represented and connected to the same CRM/business record. Spatial visibility does not replace record governance; it becomes another view of governed truth.
