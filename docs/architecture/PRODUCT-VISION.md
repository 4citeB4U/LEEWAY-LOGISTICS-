# LeeWay Logistics Product Vision

## World-first operating model

LeeWay Logistics opens on a live spatial world rather than a conventional dashboard. The operator can rotate the globe, search for an address, customer, broker, terminal, repair hub, facility, driver, vehicle, or load, and zoom directly into the operational context.

Spatial hierarchy: WORLD -> REGION -> CITY -> FACILITY/TERMINAL/CUSTOMER -> ROUTE -> VEHICLE -> DRIVER -> LOAD/CONTRACT/DOCUMENT.

Each entity preserves identity, state, provenance, permissions, timestamps, and relationships.

## CRM behavior

The CRM should use configurable boards, connected records, multiple views, workflows, activity timelines, automation rules, dashboards, forms, and account/contact relationships.

Connected records include Account, Contact, Broker, Shipper, Consignee, Facility, Driver, Employee, Candidate, Vehicle, Trailer, Load, Contract/Rate Confirmation, Route, Appointment, Incident, Work Order, Inspection, Document, Communication, Task/Reminder, and Receipt.

A record may appear in table, kanban, timeline, calendar, map, world, dashboard, and detail views without duplicating truth.
